# UI5 — Glass Ledger

## Scope and mode

Operate. Existing Thai personal ledger, mobile first; reskin all screens and sheets without changing finance rules. User approved the integrated balance/chart and now requests iPhone-like translucency and capture only on demand. No native-iPhone claim. Preserve all features, storage and user content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Money stays visible; recording is one intentional tap away. Remove the permanently open form, not its capabilities. A single persistent labelled capture button opens the existing form and focuses typing synchronously.

OWN-WORLD: Purple-black ground, mint actions and warm coral expenses. Stationary tinted light fields show through restrained glass panels, with readable mostly-opaque fields and sheets. Shared Thai font and controls across history, calendar, accounts, settings and every dialog. No emoji or decorative raster.

STORY: Read the real current balance and explore the chart. Tap จดรายการ in the thumb zone, type immediately, confirm account only when needed. Close safely with draft intact. Daily history still supports recording on the selected date.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact header, full-width balance/chart card; useful frequent and pinned records below / beside it on desktop. No inline capture form. Glass navigation dock below a single mint recording action. Mobile sheets remain scrollable above the keyboard, desktop sheets centered. Labels, totals and long values must remain readable at 320px.

FORM: User-pinned 024014/024119 chart composition plus requested iPhone translucent material, expanded into the incumbent app. Code-led, no concept tournament: palette, graph and recording interaction are explicitly specified. Native dialog/top layer provides protected focus and backdrop rather than fake floating forms.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
